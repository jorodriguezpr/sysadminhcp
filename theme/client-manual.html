Chapter 1

1.Getting Started

1.1 Logging in

Your hosting provider gives you a Client Portal URL (usually https://yourserver:7777/display or a custom address they provide), along with a username and password. Open the URL in your browser and sign in with those credentials.

If your provider has enabled Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) on your account, you'll be asked for a 6-digit code from your authenticator app after entering your password. See Section 22.3 to set this up yourself if it isn't already enabled.

1.2 Portal layout & navigation

The left sidebar is your main menu, grouped into sections:

GroupContains
OverviewDashboard
HostingDomains, Subdomains, DNS Records, Email, Webmail, FTP, Databases, phpMyAdmin
SecuritySSL, Let's Encrypt
ToolsFile Manager, Backups, PHP Version, Bandwidth, App InstallerPro, Email MigrationPro
AnalyticsAI Spam Filter (only visible if enabled for you)
SupportSupport Tickets Pro
AccountMy Account, About

The header bar at the top shows the current page title, a notification bell (Pro accounts), your username, and a Logout button. On mobile devices, tap the hamburger icon (☰) to open the sidebar.

Tip — Quick Links The Dashboard has a grid of Quick Link tiles (Domains, Email, DNS, FTP, Databases, phpMyAdmin, SSL, Files, Backups) that jump straight to each section — handy shortcuts once you're logged in.

1.3 Feature permissions & hidden menu items

Not every account can use every feature. Your administrator controls, per account, which actions you're allowed to perform (adding domains, adding mailboxes, deleting FTP accounts, managing DNS, etc.), as well as numeric limits (maximum number of domains, mailboxes, FTP accounts, databases, disk space and bandwidth).

If a button doesn't work If you click a "Create" or "Delete" button and get a permission error, or a menu item you expect to see is missing entirely, your administrator has most likely restricted that feature on your account, or you've hit a resource limit. Contact your administrator to review your account's permissions and limits.

Similarly, some sidebar items only appear when applicable:

  • AI Spam Filter — only shows if your administrator has turned on AI-based spam filtering for at least one of your domains.
  • Support Tickets, Notifications bell, App Installer, Email Migration — these are Pro features. They only appear if your hosting account is on a Pro license.

1.4 Appearance: themes, font size & density

Under My Account you'll find an Appearance/Theme section where you can personalize how the portal looks — purely visual, saved to your browser, and doesn't affect your hosting in any way:

  • Theme: Cosmic (default), Classic, Dark, Ocean, Sunset, Forest
  • Font Size: Small, Medium, Large
  • Density: Compact, Cozy, Comfortable

Chapter 2

2.Dashboard & Resource Usage

The Dashboard is your home page after logging in. It shows:

  • A Quick Links grid for jumping directly to any major feature.
  • A Resource Usage card with a usage bar for each of the following, showing how much you've used against your account's limit:
    • Disk Space — calculated from your home directory and mailbox storage
    • Bandwidth — calculated for your current billing cycle
    • Domains, Mail Accounts, FTP Accounts, Databases — counts against your account limits

Each usage bar changes color as you approach your limit: green under 70%, orange between 70–90%, and red above 90%. If a limit shows as ∞, that resource is unlimited on your account.

No "plan" picker Unlike some hosting panels, there is no self-service plan-upgrade page in the Client Portal — your resource limits are individual settings on your account configured by your administrator. If you need more space, domains, or mailboxes, contact your administrator (or open a support ticket, if enabled — see Section 18).

Chapter 3

3.Domains

The Domains page lists every domain on your account with its Type, Status, PHP version, and whether SSL is active (✓/✗).

3.1 Adding a domain

  1. Click + Add Domain in the top-right toolbar.
  2. Enter the Domain Name (e.g. example.com).
  3. Choose a Type: Virtual (a normal, independent website), Addon, or Forward (redirects to another URL).
  4. Click Create.
Domain limit reached? If you see "Domain limit reached", your account has hit its maximum number of domains. Contact your administrator to raise the limit.

3.2 Editing a domain

Click Edit on any domain row to open its settings, organized into four groups:

GroupSettings
GeneralDocument Root (default /home/{you}/{domain}), Index File (default index.html)
Forward (Forward-type domains only)Forward URL, Forward Type (301 Permanent / 302 Temporary)
WebSSL Enabled, PHP Version (5.6 – 8.4), PHP Mode (PHP-FPM, mod_php, suPHP, FCGId, CGI, LSAPI), Force HTTPS
MailCatchall Enabled/Disabled, Catchall Email address
DNSDNS Managed (Yes/No)

Make your changes and click Save.

3.3 Advanced: VHost, SSL VHost & Webmail VHost editors

Each domain row also has three advanced buttons that open the raw web-server configuration for that domain:

  • VHost — the plain HTTP (port 80) Apache/Nginx virtual host configuration.
  • SSL VHost — the HTTPS (port 443) virtual host. Includes a Generate Template button that fills in a working boilerplate block for you.
  • Webmail VHost — the configuration for webmail.{domain}.
Advanced users only These editors let you directly edit the live server configuration text for your domain. An invalid configuration can take your site offline. Only use these if you understand Apache/Nginx configuration syntax, or if your administrator has instructed you to make a specific change here.

3.4 Viewing visitor statistics

Click Stats on a domain row to open its traffic report in a new tab. If no report has been generated yet, you'll see a "No stats report available yet" message.

3.5 Deleting a domain

Click Delete on a domain row and confirm. This permanently removes the domain and all associated data (files, mail, databases tied to it, DNS records) — this action cannot be undone, so make sure you have a backup first (see Section 14).

Chapter 4

4.Subdomains

Subdomains (like shop.example.com or blog.example.com) are managed on their own page.

  1. Click + Add Subdomain.
  2. Enter the Subdomain Name (just the prefix, e.g. shop).
  3. Choose the Parent Domain from the dropdown.
  4. Click Create. Your new subdomain will be shop.example.com.

To remove one, click Delete on its row and confirm.

Chapter 5

5.DNS Records

If you only have one domain, the DNS Records page opens straight into that domain's record list. With multiple domains, you'll first see a list of DNS zones — click Manage Records on the one you want to edit.

Adding a record

  1. Click + Add Record.
  2. Enter the Hostname (use @ for the root domain, or a prefix like www).
  3. Choose the Type: A, AAAA, CNAME, MX, TXT, or NS.
  4. Enter the Content (an IP address for A/AAAA, or a hostname for CNAME/MX/NS).
  5. Set the TTL in seconds (default 3600).
  6. If Type is MX, also set a Priority (default 10 — lower numbers are tried first).
  7. Click Add.

Editing or deleting a record

Click Edit on any record row to change its Type, Host, Value, TTL, Priority, or Weight (the edit form additionally supports SRV and CAA record types). Click Delete and confirm to remove a record.

Bulk-updating all A records ("Change IP")

If your server's IP address changes and you need to update every A record in a zone at once:

  1. Click 🌐 Change IP at the top of the zone view.
  2. Enter the New IP Address.
  3. Click Update All A Records — every A record in that zone is updated in one step.

Chapter 6

6.Email

The Email page has five tabs: Mail Accounts, Mail Domains, Forwards, Spam Whitelist, and Spam Blacklist.

6.1 Mail Accounts

This tab lists every mailbox on your account with its domain, quota, and status.

Creating a mailbox

  1. Click + Add Account.
  2. Enter the Email Address (just the part before the @).
  3. Choose the Domain.
  4. Set a Password.
  5. Set a Quota in MB (enter 0 for unlimited).
  6. Click Create.

To change a mailbox's password, click Password on its row. To remove a mailbox entirely (including its stored mail), click Delete and confirm. You can also click Webmail on any row to jump straight into that domain's webmail login.

6.2 Mail Domains

A read-only list of which of your domains have mail enabled, and their catchall address status.

6.3 Forwards

A forward automatically redirects incoming mail for one address to another address, without creating a real mailbox.

  1. Click + Add Forward.
  2. Enter the Source Address (e.g. sales@example.com).
  3. Enter the Destination (e.g. me@gmail.com).
  4. Choose whether to Keep Copy — Yes delivers to a local mailbox as well as forwarding; No forwards only.
  5. Click Create.

Click Delete on a forward's row to remove it.

6.4 Spam Whitelist

Senders on your whitelist always bypass the AI spam filter for your mailboxes.

  1. Choose a Scope: My Domain (applies to every mailbox on a domain) or My Mailbox (applies to one address only).
  2. Choose an Entry Type: Email address or Domain (all @).
  3. Fill in the Target (which domain or mailbox this rule applies to) and the Value (the sender email or sender domain to whitelist).
  4. Click + Add.

The table shows who added each entry — Admin, You, or One-click link (added via a quarantine notification email link). Click Remove to delete an entry.

6.5 Spam Blacklist

Senders on your blacklist are instantly marked as spam without being analyzed — this also saves AI processing cost. Works identically to the whitelist above (same Scope / Entry Type / Target / Value fields).

Ownership is always checked You can only add whitelist/blacklist entries and forwards for domains and mailboxes you actually own — the portal will reject entries for domains that aren't on your account.

7.Webmail

This page lists a webmail login link for each of your domains (https://webmail.{domain}/) — click any link to open that domain's webmail in a new tab and log in with a mailbox address and password.

If your server has both Roundcube and RainLoop installed, a Client dropdown appears next to each domain so you can choose which webmail application serves that domain.

8.FTP Accounts

  1. Click + Add FTP Account.
  2. Enter a Username and Password.
  3. Optionally choose a Domain to associate the account with.
  4. Set the Home Directory (pre-filled to your home folder — you can point it at a specific subfolder to restrict what this FTP account can see).
  5. Click Create.

Use the Password button on any row to reset that account's password, or Delete to remove it.

Chapter 9

9.Databases

This page has two cards: Databases and Database Users.

9.1 Creating a database

  1. Click + Add Database.
  2. Enter a Database Name.
  3. Optionally, enter a DB User Name and DB User Password to create a dedicated user for it in the same step.
  4. Click Create.
Important — your databases are automatically prefixed To keep every client's databases separate on a shared server, SysAdminHCP automatically prefixes anything you create with your account username. If your username is acme and you create a database called shop, the real database name on the server is acme_shop — and a database user webuser becomes acme_webuser. Use these full, prefixed names in your website's/application's database connection settings.

Click phpMyAdmin on any database row to open it directly in phpMyAdmin (see Section 10), or Delete to permanently remove the database and all its data.

9.2 Database users & access

Creating a standalone database user

  1. Click + Add DB User.
  2. Enter a Username and Password.
  3. Click Create.

Granting a user access to a database

  1. On the user's row, click Assign DB.
  2. Choose the Database.
  3. Choose the Privileges level:
    • ALL PRIVILEGES — full access
    • SELECT — read-only
    • Read/Write — can read and modify data, but not change the database schema
  4. Click Grant Access.

Each granted database appears as a badge on the user's row with its own Revoke button. Use the row's Password button to reset a user's password, or Delete to remove the user entirely (this revokes all their access).

Chapter 10

10.phpMyAdmin

Click 🧭 Open phpMyAdmin to log in automatically — no separate phpMyAdmin password to remember. You'll be signed in with the credentials of your own database user, and can see every database owned by your account.

How the automatic login works If you haven't created a dedicated database user yet, the portal quietly creates one for you the first time you open phpMyAdmin. The single-click login link is valid for 60 seconds and can only be used once.
"Cannot connect to MySQL" error If phpMyAdmin login fails with a message like "Cannot connect to MySQL as '...'. The database user may not exist or the password is incorrect." — go to Section 9.2 and verify your database user exists and has a valid password.

If this page instead shows "phpMyAdmin is not installed on this server" or "Your account does not have access to phpMyAdmin", contact your administrator.

Chapter 11

11.SSL Certificates

The SSL page lists every certificate installed on your domains — Domain, Type, Valid From, Expires, Auto Renew, and Status.

Requesting a free certificate This page is view-only. To actually request and install a certificate yourself, use Let's Encrypt (Section 12). There is currently no self-service option to upload your own purchased/custom SSL certificate — if you have one you need installed, contact your administrator.

Chapter 12

12.Let's Encrypt (Free SSL)

Let's Encrypt certificates are free, automatically trusted by all browsers, and renew themselves — no ongoing action needed from you once issued.

Requesting a certificate

  1. Go to Let's Encrypt in the sidebar.
  2. Under Request New Certificate, select the Domain you want to secure.
  3. Optionally enter a notification Email (if left blank, an address at your domain is used automatically).
  4. Click Request Certificate. You'll see "Requesting certificate... this may take a minute" while it's issued.
  5. Once complete, you'll see "Certificate installed successfully!" and the domain now shows SSL as active everywhere in the portal.
Before requesting Your domain's DNS must already be pointing at this server, and the domain must be reachable over plain HTTP, for Let's Encrypt's ownership verification to succeed. If DNS was only just changed, wait for it to propagate first.

The Issued Certificates table on this page shows status badges: Expired, Expiring Soon (within 30 days), or Active. Auto-renewal is always on for Let's Encrypt certificates — there is nothing further you need to do.

Chapter 13

13.File Manager

File Manager gives you full access to your own home directory (/home/{you}) and everything inside it — you cannot browse outside this folder.

Navigating

Use the breadcrumb trail at the top, or type a path directly into the path box and click Go. Click Show hidden to reveal dotfiles (like .htaccess).

Uploading, creating & organizing files

  • Upload: click ⬆ Upload and choose one or more files, or drag files directly onto the file listing (up to 200MB per file).
  • New Folder / New File: use the respective toolbar buttons and enter a name.
  • Select multiple files via the checkboxes to reveal a bulk toolbar: Copy, Cut, Download (bundles selected files into a zip), Compress, and Delete.
  • After a Copy or Cut, navigate to the destination folder and click 📋 Paste.

Per-file actions

ActionWhat it does
⬇ DownloadDownloads the file to your computer
EditOpens recognized text files (HTML, CSS, JS, PHP, config files, etc.) in a built-in editor
ExtractUnpacks a .zip, .tar, .gz, .tgz or .tar.gz archive into a folder you choose
RenameRenames the file or folder
ChmodChanges Unix permissions (e.g. 755) — for folders, you can apply this recursively
DeletePermanently deletes the file or folder

To compress files, select them and click Compress, name the archive, and choose ZIP or TAR.GZ.

Chapter 14

14.Backups

You can create, download, restore, and delete your own account backups at any time — no need to wait for your administrator.

Creating a backup

  1. Click + Create Backup.
  2. By default, Full Backup (all components) is checked — this backs up everything. Uncheck it to pick specific components instead:
    • 🏠 Home Files — all files in your home directory
    • ⚙️ Account Settings — domains, DNS zones, mail accounts, FTP users, database records
    • 📧 Email Data — mailbox contents for all your domains
    • 🔒 SSL Certificates — certificate and key files
    • 🗄️ Databases — MySQL/MariaDB dumps
    • 📂 FTP Users — FTP account configuration
    • 🌐 DNS Zone Files
    • 🖥️ Apache Config — virtual host files
    • 📬 Webmail Config
  3. Click 🚀 Create Backup. A progress bar tracks the job (this runs in the background, so you can navigate elsewhere and come back).

Downloading, restoring & deleting

Each backup in the list shows its contents, size, and creation date, with three actions:

  • ⬇ Download — saves the backup archive (.tgz) to your computer.
  • 🔄 Restore — opens a confirmation showing what's in the backup. Check Skip existing records if you only want to restore what's missing without overwriting current data, then click 🔄 Restore Now.
  • 🗑 Delete — permanently removes the backup file from the server.
Restoring overwrites data Unless you check "Skip existing records", restoring a backup will overwrite your current domains, mail accounts, and other settings with what's in the backup, and databases are recreated from the backup's SQL dumps. Only restore when you're sure this is what you want.

Chapter 17

17.AI Spam Filter & Email Log

This menu item only appears if your administrator has enabled AI-based spam filtering on at least one of your domains. It shows, for the last 30 days: Emails Analyzed, Spam Blocked, Ham (legitimate mail) Delivered, and your Spam Rate percentage — plus a breakdown of AI token usage by day, month, and year.

17.1 Email Log — Recent Entries

Below the stats tiles is an Email Log card — a record of every individual email that reached your domain(s), showing exactly who it was from, who it was addressed to, its subject, and whether it was delivered to the inbox or quarantined as spam.

Proof an email actually arrived This is the place to check whenever you (or one of your own clients) believe an expected email "never arrived." Search the log for the sender or subject — if it's there, you'll see immediately whether it was delivered normally or caught by the spam filter, instead of just taking "I never got it" at face value.
  1. Go to AI Spam Filter in the sidebar.
  2. If you have more than one domain with the filter enabled, choose which one to view from the domain dropdown (or leave it on "All my domains").
  3. Click ↻ Load Recent Entries.
  4. Use the search box to filter the list by sender, recipient, or subject — handy when you're looking for one specific email rather than scrolling the whole list.

Each row shows:

ColumnMeaning
DateWhen the email was received and processed
FromThe sender's email address
ToThe mailbox on your domain it was delivered to
SubjectThe email's subject line
Status✓ Delivered — reached the inbox normally, or 🚫 Quarantined — held as spam (hover the badge to see why, e.g. "phishing", "marketing")
ConfidenceHow confident the AI filter was in that verdict
Only covers domains with AI filtering enabled The Email Log only has data for domains where your administrator has turned on AI spam filtering — it isn't a general record of every email your server has ever handled. If a domain doesn't appear in the dropdown, or the whole AI Spam Filter menu item is missing, filtering isn't enabled for it; ask your administrator if you'd like this turned on.

18.Support Tickets Pro

If your hosting account is on a Pro license, you can open and track support tickets directly from the portal.

Opening a ticket

  1. Click + New Ticket.
  2. Enter a Subject.
  3. Choose a Department.
  4. Choose a Priority: Low, Medium (default), High, or Urgent.
  5. Optionally select a Related Domain.
  6. Write your Message describing the issue.
  7. Click Submit Ticket.

Replying to a ticket

Click any ticket in your list to open it. Support staff replies appear on a light-blue background labeled "Support Staff"; your own replies appear in gray. If the ticket is still open, type your reply in the box at the bottom and click Send Reply. Closed tickets show "This ticket is closed" instead of a reply box — open a new ticket if you need further help on the same topic.

Chapter 20

20.App Installer Pro

Install common web applications onto a domain without manual setup.

  1. Click New Install (or similarly-labeled button).
  2. Choose the Application to install.
  3. Choose the Domain (subdomains aren't listed here — install onto the main domain).
  4. Optionally set an Install Path to install into a subfolder rather than the domain root.
  5. Submit — the install list auto-refreshes every 5 seconds while your install is in progress (status moves from pending → installing → completed/failed).

Click a completed install to see its site URL and any post-install instructions. Use Delete to remove an install — note this removes the installed files from your hosting account.

Chapter 21

21.Email Migration Pro

Bring your mailbox over from your old email host. SysAdminHCP connects to your old provider over IMAP and copies your messages straight into a mailbox here — your old account is never modified.

  1. Click New Import.
  2. Under Your Old Mailbox (IMAP), enter the old provider's IMAP Host, Port (default 993), whether to use TLS (default Yes), and your old Email/Username and Password.
  3. Under Your New Mailbox, choose the destination Domain and Email. You can set a New Mailbox Password, or leave it blank to auto-generate one (only used if the mailbox doesn't already exist).
  4. Submit the import.
Your old password is never stored Your old mailbox's password is used only once, for this import, and is not saved anywhere afterward.

The import list shows live progress (migrated/total messages, current folder) and auto-refreshes every few seconds while running. Deleting an import from the list does not remove any mail already copied over.

Chapter 22

22.My Account

22.1 Profile

Update your Real Name, Email, Phone, Language (English/Español/Português), Address, City, State and Country, then click Save Changes. Your Username and Login Type are fixed and cannot be changed here.

22.2 Changing your password

  1. Enter your Current Password.
  2. Enter a New Password.
  3. Click Change Password.

22.3 Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

2FA adds a second security step (a rotating 6-digit code from an app like Google Authenticator or Authy) on top of your password.

Enabling 2FA

  1. Click Enable 2FA.
  2. Scan the QR code shown with your authenticator app (or type in the manual-entry code if you can't scan).
  3. Enter the current 6-digit code from the app and click Verify & Enable.
  4. You'll be shown a set of one-time-use backup codes — save these somewhere safe now, as they will not be shown again. Use a backup code to log in if you ever lose access to your authenticator app.

Disabling 2FA

Click Disable 2FA and confirm with your current password.

22.4 API Access

Generate an API key to call the SysAdminHCP API from your own scripts or tools, without exposing your account password.

  1. Click Generate New Key.
  2. Enter a Label to remember what it's for, and optionally an expiry in Days.
  3. Click Generate — the raw key is shown once. Copy it immediately; it cannot be retrieved again later.

To use the key, exchange it for a temporary access token by calling POST /api/auth/api-login with {"apiKey":"..."} — the key itself cannot be used to log into the portal directly. Revoke a key at any time from its row; this never affects your account password.

Chapter 23

23.Troubleshooting & FAQ

"I don't have permission" / a button doesn't do anything

Your administrator can restrict individual actions (adding domains, deleting mailboxes, managing DNS, etc.) per account. This is expected behavior, not a bug — contact your administrator to review your permissions.

A menu item I expect to see is missing

Some sections only appear when relevant: AI Spam Filter requires it to be enabled on at least one domain; Support Tickets, the notification bell, App Installer, and Email Migration all require a Pro license.

My database isn't named what I typed

Databases and database users you create are automatically prefixed with your account username ({username}_{name}) — see Section 9.1. Use the full prefixed name in your application's connection settings.

phpMyAdmin says it can't connect to MySQL

Check that your database user exists and has a working password under Databases → Database Users.

I hit a limit (domains, mailboxes, disk space, bandwidth...)

These are individual account limits set by your administrator, visible as usage bars on the Dashboard. There's no self-service upgrade — contact your administrator or open a support ticket to request a higher limit.

I can't find a way to upload my own SSL certificate

Self-service SSL is currently limited to free Let's Encrypt certificates (Section 12). If you need a purchased/custom certificate installed, ask your administrator.

File Manager won't let me go outside my home folder

This is intentional — client accounts are confined to their own home directory (/home/{username}) for security.

A client (or I) says an email was never received

Check the Email Log under AI Spam Filter — search for the sender or subject and see whether it actually arrived and was delivered, or was caught and quarantined as spam. If the domain doesn't have AI spam filtering enabled, there's no log to check yet; ask your administrator to enable it.
